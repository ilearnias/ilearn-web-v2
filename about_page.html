About Us

Empowering Dreams, Nurturing Leaders

iLearn IAS has redefined the coaching industry with its innovative methodologies. Recognizing each student’s uniqueness, it pioneered the concept of Personal Mentorship, offering tailored guidance to address individual strengths and challenges. This personalized approach has made iLearn a leader in civil services coaching.

The institution has also introduced the ‘Intelligent Knowledge Appraiser (IKA)’ in its Exam Platform to identify and address students' weak points effectively.

The Directors—Nikhil Lohithakshan, Mohammed Shinas, TJ Abraham, and Dias Jose—envisioned an institution that sees civil service not just as a profession but as a tool for national transformation. Their philosophy inspires students to embrace their roles as changemakers in society.
